# Mission

## Goal

Build a personal brand website that positions Rayner Villalba as a credible, interesting engineer in the tech community — not just a job seeker.

## Primary Audience

- Peers in the tech community
- Conference organizers and speakers
- Open-source collaborators
- (Secondary) Recruiters and potential clients who discover the site through the above channels

## What Success Looks Like

A visitor lands on the site and immediately understands:
1. Who Rayner is and what he stands for
2. What he has built and what problems he solves
3. How to reach him or follow his work

The site should feel like a craftsman made it — not a template. It should reflect technical taste and design sensibility, not just list credentials.

## Scope Boundaries

- **Content:** Hero, Work Experience, Projects, Contact. No blog, no skills grid, no certifications in v1.
- **Content management:** Code-only. No CMS, no admin panel.
- **Interactivity:** Scroll animations only. No contact form, no PDF export in v1.
- **Deployment:** Static hosting (Netlify or Vercel). No server-side code.
